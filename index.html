Himanshu Singh

From the press box to the latent space. AI × Products × Content × Growth.

01 / THE SHIFT narrative × leverage

I spent years writing sports on unforgiving, real-time match deadlines—distilling chaos, tactical swings, and high-pressure moments into stories readers couldn’t abandon.

"In the press box, you don't have the luxury of analysis paralysis. You synthesize fast, isolate the inflection point, and ship with 90 seconds on the clock."

That background is an unfair advantage in tech. Today, language models are narrative engines. Products win on positioning, emotional resonance, and organic distribution loops long before features:

  • Narrative Gravity: Great positioning cuts through noise. Software is adopted when users feel immediate emotional clarity.
  • Press-Box Velocity: Rapid hypothesis testing, zero perfectionism, and tight feedback loops with real users.
  • AI as Content & Product Leverage: Building multi-agent workflows, contextual evaluation guardrails, and organic acquisition loops.
